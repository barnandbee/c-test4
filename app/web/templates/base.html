Australian university pay scales

Roles are usually advertised against one of two scales. The exact salary for a level varies by institution (each has its own enterprise agreement), so treat these as a guide, not a quote — always check the listing.

Professional / general staff — HEW

“HEW” = Higher Education Worker (some institutions say HEO/HEP). Levels 1–10.

  • HEW 1–3 — entry / administrative & support
  • HEW 4–5 — experienced officer, coordinator support
  • HEW 6–7 — officer / coordinator / advisor (degree + experience)
  • HEW 8–9 — senior specialist / manager
  • HEW 10 — senior manager / director (above 10 = executive)

Academic — Levels A–E

  • Level A — Associate Lecturer / Postdoctoral Fellow
  • Level B — Lecturer
  • Level C — Senior Lecturer
  • Level D — Associate Professor
  • Level E — Professor

Tip: the pay level often tells you more about seniority and responsibility than the dollar figure alone — many roles list only the level.
